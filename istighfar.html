Android application

Istighfar Counter

A calm, distraction-free way to keep up with daily istighfar. Every feature is free, with no ads, subscriptions or account required.

  • Count with screen taps or volume keys.
  • Use a fully black, dimmed Pocket screen. Tap to count; swipes are ignored. Exit with Android Back.
  • See daily history, goals and week-to-week or month-to-month comparisons.
  • Choose your phrase, reading aids and theme.
  • Export and restore a backup of your counts and settings.
  • Keep counting records on your device and use the app offline.

Optional advanced screen-off volume counting is available through Shizuku. It requires separate setup; ordinary counting and Pocket Mode work without it.

The Android app is being prepared for release. A public download link will be added here when available.
